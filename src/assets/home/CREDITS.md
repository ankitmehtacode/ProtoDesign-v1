# Hero film (src/assets/hero-climb/)

Every frame is rendered in Blender (EEVEE) by the ProtoDesign team, 2026-09-29, from
ProtoDesign's own scene (Documents/protodesign-hero/hero-climb.blend and
hero-climb-tall.blend, kept outside the repo). The motion and camera come from
scripts/hero-climb/animate.py and camera.py; scripts/hero-climb/encode.sh turns the
renders into the frame sets. Reflections are lit with the Poly Haven HDRI
"studio_small_09" (CC0, https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09).
